What's the --- block for?

Optional page header/footer text for the rendered CV — set via header:/footer:, rendered on every page, not part of the CV body. Must stay the very first lines of the document.

---
header: Jane Doe — Résumé
footer: jane@example.com · linkedin.com/in/janedoe
---
