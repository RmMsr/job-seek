How it works

  1. Sources — Job boards, Slack communities, and listing pages you register on the Sources page.
  2. Fetch — Pulls new postings from each source, skipping URLs already seen.
    1. Classify — An LLM tags each posting as a job posting, a lead, irrelevant, or an error. Irrelevant postings are discarded rather than stored.
    2. Summarize — Job postings and leads get an AI-written title, headline, and summary.
    3. Scenario alignment — For each active scenario, the summary is evaluated against that scenario's Criteria (what you're looking for right now — must/prefer/avoid, refined over time) to produce a relevance score. Clearing at least one scenario's gate threshold is what makes a posting count as relevant.
    4. Profile fit — Postings that clear a scenario's gate are also evaluated against your Profile (who you are — skills, interests, constraints, set once) to score interest and attainability. This fit score is used to rank the Jobs list, not to filter it.
  3. Jobs — The Jobs list surfaces everything sorted by fit score. You accept, reject, or trash, each with a short note.
    1. Refine — Your notes accumulate as feedback. The Scenarios page can propose criteria updates based on what you've been accepting and rejecting — closing the loop back into Scenario alignment.
